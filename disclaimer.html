About this website

This website presents Gravity Bakery, listed on Google as Gravity Cakes in Kodungaiyur, Chennai. Business information is based on the linked Google Business Profile and should be confirmed with the bakery when making arrangements.

Cake photographs & descriptions

The cake photographs used on this website are sourced from photos uploaded by Gravity Cakes to its Google Business Profile. No stock or AI-generated product images are used. Design names and descriptions describe the visible cakes; they are not a confirmed flavour or price menu. Photos show past work. Colours, decorations, portions and final designs may vary.

Enquiries & order confirmation

Submitting the form prepares a WhatsApp message; it does not automatically send a message, place an order, take a payment or reserve a date. Confirm the design, flavour, size, price, payment terms, timing, collection or delivery, and any cancellation arrangements directly with the bakery before proceeding.

Ingredients & dietary requirements

Please ask the bakery directly about ingredients, allergens, eggless options and preparation arrangements. Do not assume a cake is suitable for a dietary requirement based on its photograph or description.

External services

WhatsApp and Google Maps are external services and operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Their availability may depend on your device, connection and location. This website does not confirm availability on third-party delivery platforms.
