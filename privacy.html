How this website handles your enquiry details.

The enquiry form

The form uses the information you enter to prepare a WhatsApp message in your browser. This website does not submit form entries to a database, email provider or form-processing service, and does not save them in browser storage.

Continuing to WhatsApp

When you continue to WhatsApp, your entered details are included in the message link and shared with WhatsApp. You can review and edit the message there before sending it to the bakery. Information you send is then handled by the bakery and WhatsApp under their own practices. Include only details needed for your enquiry.

Google Maps & hosting

The contact page embeds Google Maps. Loading the map connects to Google, which may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details and may use cookies under its own policies. The hosting provider may also process technical request information to serve and secure the website.

Analytics & advertising

This version of the website does not include custom analytics scripts, advertising trackers or a marketing subscription form.

Questions about your enquiry

For questions about a message you have sent or how the bakery handles it, contact the bakery directly.
