BEFORE WE START

A few things
to know.

How do I place an order?+

Choose a design and send an enquiry through WhatsApp. The bakery will discuss your cake, confirm availability and provide a quote. Your order is confirmed only when the team agrees the details with you.

Can I change the design or flavour?+

Tell the team your preferred colours, theme, message, flavour and size. The photos show previous cake designs. Ask what can be made for your date.

What about eggless cakes and allergies?+

Please discuss dietary requirements and allergies directly with the bakery before ordering. Ingredients and preparation arrangements must be confirmed; photographs do not establish whether a cake is eggless or allergen-free.

Do you offer delivery or same-day orders?+

Share your date and location with the bakery to check availability, lead time, collection options and any delivery charges.
